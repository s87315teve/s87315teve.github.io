國立陽明交通大學（NYCU）電機學院博士候選人，研究聚焦於 **人工智慧 × 無線通訊 × 無人機（UAV）**，

曾赴北海道大學大學院情報科學研究院擔任特別研究學生（2024–2025）。

Ph.D. candidate at the College of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan.
My research lies at the intersection of **AI, wireless communications, and UAVs** — air-to-ground channel
measurement and modeling, software-defined radio, and digital twins for V2X.
Special Research Student at Hokkaido University, Japan (2024–2025).
